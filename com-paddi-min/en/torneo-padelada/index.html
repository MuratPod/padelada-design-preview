Torneo Padelada

The Temporada lasts half a year

Every half year, a new story begins in Padelada. From April to September and from October to March, every Torneo Padelada, wherever it takes place, becomes a chapter of that story. Your name appears in the Temporada table, seen by everyone who plays alongside you. Tournaments that organisers run on their own terms matter too: they move your personal rating. But only Torneo Padelada count towards the Temporada table. The First Season runs from launch to the end of March. Its champions will be the first, forever.

Points

Every Torneo Padelada earns points for everyone who steps onto the court. Your best tournaments of the Temporada count. So one bad evening decides nothing, and one great day can decide everything.

Trophies

Anyone who finishes in the top three of a tournament gets a virtual trophy. And the top ten of the Temporada, in their region and overall, get a Temporada virtual trophy. Every trophy has a verification code: anyone can check it's real. It can't be bought or faked, only won.

Table and public visibility

Your name and result appear in the Temporada table only if you turn on “Public profile” in the app yourself. Without it, you play and earn points like everyone else, and the table shows “Player” instead of your name. Every score correction is saved and visible to all participants, so there's nothing to argue about with the table. We never publish minors.

How to join the standings

Find a Torneo Padelada near you in the app and sign up. The organiser sets up the tournament, and you enter the score from your iPhone or your watch. After the last match, points land by themselves in your region's table and the overall standings.
