Your club, only livelier

Players find the fourth themselves and put together the evening game. If someone drops out, they look for a replacement in the chat instead of cancelling the court.

Your club gets its own page in Padelada: courts and their descriptions, upcoming tournaments and games that are one player short, the season’s ranking and league, club champions. Booking stays yours, and the link leads straight to you.

Confirm the club is yours and it becomes official. You and your admins manage the name, logo, contacts and prices, and announcements from the club reach all of its players.

Tournaments run without the hassle. The club owner gets Organizador status right away, and the app handles pairs, scores and the table itself. A fair tournament earns the Torneo Padelada mark.

Players come to you with a level confirmed by play, so groups and evening games come together more evenly.

Booking, prices and rules stay yours. Padelada takes no money from the club.
