Club Padelada

A club that doesn't sell tickets

Most clubs sell you a place: a fee, a recommendation, an introduction at the door. At Club Padelada that doesn't work. You can't get in with money or on someone else's word. You arrive from the court.

That's why everyone here knows who they play with. Everyone you meet in the club has already played fair — with witnesses and a confirmed score. Their level is earned, not claimed.

How to get in

Play two games in 7 days with four different people. Have your photo and real name on your profile. Nothing else is needed.

No forms, no approvals, no queue: as soon as the conditions are met, you are accepted automatically. Membership stays for good — a break from playing doesn't get you removed. There is only one way to lose it: cheating.

Your card

The club member card is coloured in your level's colour, and carries your face and the date you joined the club. The colour changes only when your game changes.

Captains

The club has no management. There is no board that appoints the best and no creator who hands out titles. Leaders are chosen by the game itself.

The club lives by comarcas, and each one has its own shared chat: it exists from the very first player. Club members write in it. A newcomer gets the chat open for the first 10 days or 20 messages, which is enough to find first games and join the club. The chat of your own match is always open. The comarca captain welcomes newcomers, puts together fours, and answers when someone is looking for a partner.

Nobody appoints captains. A comarca opens once it has 16 club members. Its first captain is whoever kept the score of the comarca’s first verified game, and they are the comarca founder for life. It is a public role and unpaid. But a captain wears gold and is known by face. When a comarca grows, the captain gets assistants.

A captain leads the comarca while they keep playing. As soon as they stop, the place is offered to the next person: here only those who are on the court lead.

Captains aren't voted in or appointed through connections. The game decides: confirmed matches, an honest level and what they bring to the community. It's meritocracy in the simplest sense: the role goes to whoever earned it on the court.

Organisers

Whoever has run several real tournaments becomes an Organizador. It's recognition, not a pass: any organizer can run open tournaments for free. And when the organizer of the month opens a tournament, only Club Padelada members can sign up for the first day.

What every club member gets

Everything that makes you part of the club is free: the card and club styling of your profile, the Referee in a disputed game, early sign-up to the best organisers' tournaments, the glow of your level ring and club cards after every match.

Questions

Can I buy a place in the club?

No. A place in the club is given only by the game.

What happens if I haven't played for a long time?

You stay a club member.

Can I ask to be made a captain?

No. The game decides: the first captain is whoever kept the score of the comarca’s first verified game.

Can a captain be reported?

Yes. Every complaint is reviewed by a referee, and only the ones they confirm count. After three complaints confirmed by a referee, the Padelada team decides whether they stay captain. If not, the club offers the place to the next person.

Who keeps order in the comarca chat?

Mostly the chat itself. Club members write in it, and someone who never starts playing only reads after 10 days or 20 messages. A club member has a coloured card, a guest has a plain one, and you can see it at once. The comarca captain handles pinned messages and spam, and any message can be reported.
