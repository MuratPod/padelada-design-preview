A tournament that runs itself

The app sets the pairs. It looks at everyone’s level and keeps the strongest players from teaming up again, so a newcomer plays as an equal and partners change from round to round. Americano and Mexicano, for any number of courts.

Download on the App StoreOpen in browserapp.padelada.com

Scores are kept from a watch or a phone right on the court. After the game, the players confirm it and the table updates for everyone at once. No scraps of paper, no tallying at the end of the night.

The last word is yours. A dispute is settled with one tap, a score can be corrected until the tournament closes, and every edit is visible to all participants.

Order holds without phone calls. Players tap “I’m here”, a spot that frees up goes to the first person on the waiting list by itself, and the organiser picks the court mode: leaders on court 1, or courts in rotation. In the tournament group everyone writes in their own language and reads in their own.

A fair tournament earns the Torneo Padelada mark. Winners receive digital awards, and the authenticity of each one can be checked by its code.

Courts, prices and rules remain yours. Padelada takes no money for the tournament.

Any player can create a tournament for free, and any organiser can run open tournaments for free. After three tournaments created, the Organizador status comes, also free: it is recognition, not a pass and not a paid feature.

The Torneo Padelada mark is free. Requirements: at least 8 players, pairs by level, every score confirmed, real names and photos for everyone, and no open disputes. Padelada doesn’t run tournaments and doesn’t take money.
