You'll find a fourth

It's Thursday evening, the court is booked, and there are only three of you. Usually what follows is dozens of messages in a group chat, voice notes, an endless "who's in?" and silence. In Padelada, all you have to do is say you're one short. Your friends from the group and players of your level nearby will see the invitation, and whoever answers first takes the spot. As soon as there are four of you, the match is already created, and after the game you simply swap QR codes.

Speak your own language

On the court it doesn't matter where you're from. But in the group chat where games are arranged, language suddenly means everything. Someone writes in Catalan, someone replies in English, and the newcomer stays quiet because they aren't sure they'll be understood.

In Padelada everyone writes in their own language and reads in their own. A message from your neighbour in Palafrugell reaches you already in English, and they read your reply in Catalan. Padel words like bandeja and víbora stay as they are, because everyone understands them anyway. If you like, the original is always one tap away.

You can record a voice message on the go, on your way to the court. The other person reads it in their own language.

Every comarca has its own chat, from its very first player. That’s where people look for a fourth, put together an evening game and get to know the ones they’ll be on court with tomorrow. You can write there straight away, and two games in 7 days will make you a club member. Next to it sits the group for your tournament, and private messages open separately, just like in any messenger you already use.

A level you can trust

Elsewhere, people name their own level, or pay a coach to get one. In Padelada, a level can be neither declared nor bought. The questionnaire at sign-up gives only a starting point; after that, only matches speak.

Every game counts once the players have confirmed it: right on the court, from their own phones, with a QR code. If not everyone agrees, the game doesn’t go into the rating.

One level across all clubs and cities: wherever you step onto a court, they already know you.

A club that doesn't sell tickets

You can't get in here through connections or for money. You earn your place on the court: two games in 7 days with four different people, and you become a club member for life. There is no management here. A comarca opens once it has 16 club members. Its first captain is whoever kept the score of its first verified game, and they are the comarca founder for life. Here, people know who they're playing with and remember every match.

Everything that matters in Padelada is free, now and forever: level, rating with its full history, club and chat. No fees to play and no ads.

Captains aren't voted in or appointed through connections. The game decides: confirmed matches, an honest level and what they bring to the community. It's meritocracy in the simplest sense: the role goes to whoever earned it on the court.

A tournament where the game wins

In Torneo Padelada, nobody's level is set by eye; it's earned on the court, match after match. That's why the pairs here are even. A beginner will play on equal terms. The strong players can't agree in advance to team up. The app builds pairs by level, and the organiser picks the mode: mixed pairs, same-gender pairs or open.

The organiser picks the court mode: leaders on court 1, or courts in rotation.

The organiser can forget about spreadsheets and scraps of paper. Partners rotate from round to round, the watch or the phone keeps score, and if someone doesn't show up, the first in the queue takes their place.

And the evening doesn't vanish without a trace. The win stays in your rating and in the season table, and whoever came alone leaves with people they'll want to meet on the court again.

Play with your watch

The match is created on your phone, and the watch does the rest: workout, score and match sheet. Your phone stays in the bag.

  • PADEL workout

    Create the match on your phone and the workout opens on your watch: time, heart rate with zone, kcal and rings.

  • Score one swipe away

    Swipe left to keep score. One tap after every rally and the point is in.

  • Crown back: 0:0

    Lost track of the points? Turn the crown back and the game goes back to 0:0.

  • Are you 3 or 4?

    Pick your spot on court as the match starts. Your side comes from your profile; one tap changes it.

  • Same pairs?

    After each set, keep the pairs or switch partners. Games count for each player.

  • Match sheet

    One player fixes the score and pairs set by set, the others confirm. Everyone is rated on their own sets.

A tournament that organises itself

Anyone who has ever run an Americano knows: the hardest part isn't the game, it's everything before it. Who plays with whom, on which court, so that nobody ends up with the same partner twice in a row, and so the strongest players don't all land in one pair. In Padelada, you give the time, the club and the format, and the app takes care of the lineup: it considers everyone's level, the side they play on, and changes partners from round to round. If someone doesn't show up, their place goes automatically to the first person on the waiting list. You can run a tournament for your own crowd, open it to everyone, or make it part of Torneo Padelada, and in every case the result goes fairly into the participants' ratings.
