Play and get a rating

Back to all guides

Your rating only changes with games other players have confirmed. There are two ways.

Way 1: “Playing now”

  1. Home → “Playing now”. The timer starts. Add your partner and opponents and pick the club.
  2. Keep score by tapping the pair, on the phone or on Apple Watch.
  3. “Finish”: the game goes to your diary. It doesn't count for the rating yet.
  4. “Mark who you played with”: show the QR and the other three scan it with their camera. The code changes every 30 seconds, so it can only be scanned on court.
  5. When all four have scanned and nobody disputes it, the rating counts at the time shown.

Way 2: “Create match with QR”

  1. Matches → “Create match with QR”. Show the QR or tap “Share match”.
  2. The others join through the QR: in the app, in the browser, or with “Join as guest” (a guest's level doesn't change).
  3. After the game, one player taps “Enter score”.
  4. The opposing pair confirms: “Confirm 6:4”. The partner of whoever entered the score doesn't need to confirm. The match never confirms by itself: it waits for the opponents.

If something goes wrong

  • The score is wrong. “Dispute the score” or “Enter a different score (starts a dispute)”. Then everyone votes: 3 votes out of 4 are needed. At 2:2 the match is played but doesn't count for the level. For a rules question, “Ask the Referee”.
  • No internet on court. The score stays on the phone. “Not sent”: it goes by itself once there's signal. “Sending”: on its way to the server. “Saved”: it's on the server.
  • The watch doesn't respond. “Open on watch”. If the watch has no app: Watch → Padelada → Install.
  • Not everyone scanned in time. The game stays in the diary, without a rating.
